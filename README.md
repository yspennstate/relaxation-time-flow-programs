# The relaxation time is not the value of a flow program

Yitzchak Shmalo

Aldous and Fill (*Reversible Markov Chains and Random Walks on Graphs*, Open Problem 3.44) ask whether the relaxation time tau_2 = 1/(1 - lambda_2) of a reversible Markov chain has a characterization as exactly an infimum over flows. Their Chapter 4 bounds tau_2 above by minima of congestion functionals over flows.

The paper shows that no characterization of that kind exists. The optimal value of a linear or convex quadratic program in flow variables, with coefficients rational in the transition probabilities, lies in the field generated by the transition probabilities. The simple random walk on the path with six vertices has rational transition probabilities and

    tau_2 = 3 + sqrt(5).

The paper also shows that tau_2 is an optimum of the objective dual to minimum congestion. With N(d) = sum_{a,b} pi_a pi_b d(a,b) and C(d) = sum over edges of c_ij d(i,j), the minimum congestion of the uniform multicommodity flow is the maximum of N(d) over semimetrics with C(d) = 1, and 2 tau_2 is the maximum of N(d) over squared Euclidean distances with C(d) = 1. The first cone is polyhedral and the second is not.

## Contents

- `paper/main.tex`, `paper/main.pdf`: the paper.
- `code/checks.py`: exact relaxation times of the simple random walks on P5, P6, C5, K4 and the Petersen graph from the characteristic polynomial, with their minimal polynomials; the congestion and energy programs on the paths (exact) and on C5 (as a linear program); the variational identity for squared Euclidean configurations; irrationality of tau_2 on 60 random integer-weighted graphs. Output in `code/checks_out.txt` and `code/checks.json`.

## Running

    cd code
    python checks.py > checks_out.txt

The program needs `sympy`, `numpy` and `scipy` and runs in about a minute.
